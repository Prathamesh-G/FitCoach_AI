# FitCoach AI


Core Features

Personalized Onboarding & Weekly Diet Schedule: Automatically adjusts daily meal guidance for mixed weekly schedules (such as Mon/Tue/Thu/Sat Veg and Wed/Fri/Sun Non-Veg), Full Vegetarian, Eggetarian, or Non-Vegetarian plans.

AI Meal Photo & Text Logger: Snap a photo of Indian plates (roti, dal, paneer, chicken, rice, thali) or describe meals in plain text to estimate portions and macros across Breakfast, Lunch, Snack, and Dinner—with in-place editing anytime.

Nutrition Label Scanner & Packaged Lookup: Scan nutrition facts labels or look up packaged foods with quick serving multipliers (0.5x to 2x).

Custom Meal Shortcuts: Save frequent homemade or packaged dishes for one-tap daily logging.

AI Meal & 5-Day Muscle Split Planner: Generates day-aware Indian meal plans plus a 5-day workout rotation (Chest/Triceps, Back/Biceps, Legs, Shoulders/Traps, and Upper Body/Arms) with Core and Cardio blocks.

Workout Logger & PR Analyzer: Log weights (kg) and reps per exercise to automatically track Top Weight PRs, Rep PRs, Peak Volume, and Estimated 1RM.

Activity Sync & Multi-Device Backup: Track steps, active burn, water, and daily habits, and back up data via local storage, JSON export, or Gmail / Google Drive sync.
