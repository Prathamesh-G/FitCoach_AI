Add iOS App Shortcut

One-tap access from your iPhone Home Screen

  1. 1 Open the MettleMap website in Safari on your iPhone.
  2. 2 Tap Safari’s Share button (the square with an upward arrow). It is usually in the bottom toolbar.
  3. 3 In the Share menu, choose “Add to Home Screen”, then tap Add. The shortcut will appear on your Home Screen.

Sharing with Friends & Family: Just share this link! Every new visitor gets their own welcome setup screen and private cloud profile automatically.

Add MettleMap to Android

Create a Home Screen shortcut

  1. 1 Open the MettleMap website in Google Chrome on your Android phone. The site must be hosted on a secure HTTPS link.
  2. 2 Tap Chrome's ⋮ menu in the top-right corner.
  3. 3 Choose “Add to Home screen” or “Install app” (the wording depends on Chrome and the website), then confirm.

If you only see “Add to Home screen”, this creates a browser shortcut. A full installable PWA requires a web app manifest and service worker on the hosted site.
